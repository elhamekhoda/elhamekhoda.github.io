I am an Assistant Professor of Teaching in the Computer Science Department at the University of British Columbia Vancouver campus. I am a member of the UBC Master of Data Science (MDS) academic team and teach several data science courses in the MDS program. With a background in particle physics, I specialize in developing Machine Learning (ML) algorithms for both offline and real-time data analysis, bridging the gap between cutting-edge computational techniques and impactful scientific discovery.

I am passionate about making education accessible to everyone, regardless of economic or geographic barriers. I actively create open educational resources that promote data science and AI literacy worldwide. If you would like to learn more about our work or explore collaboration opportunities, feel free to reach out.
